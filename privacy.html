Privacy Policy — Layout Call

Last updated: November 11, 2026 (America/Toronto). Terms subject to update.

This policy explains how Kyle Seguin, operating as Layout Call (“we” or “Layout Call”), handles personal information when we answer phone calls for our business clients (trade and home-service businesses in Ontario) using an AI receptionist. We follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Who we are

Kyle Seguin, operating as Layout Call, PO Box 1092, 69 Minto Street, Chapleau, ON P0M 1K0, Ontario, Canada.

Privacy contact: Kyle Seguin, at the mailing address above. Email support details are coming soon.

You are speaking with an AI

When you call one of our clients and the call is answered by our service, you are speaking with an AI assistant, not a person. The assistant says so at the start of the call and whenever you ask.

What we collect

  • Your phone number and the time and length of your call.
  • What you tell the assistant, for example your name, callback number, service address, the problem, how urgent it is, and your preferred appointment time.
  • A recording and a written transcript of the call.
  • Basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

Why we collect it

  • To answer the business’s phone, take your message or booking request, and pass it to the business.
  • To try to connect you to the business owner in an emergency.
  • To review call quality and fix mistakes in the assistant.
  • To handle complaints and disputes.

We do not sell your information, use it for marketing, or use your call recordings to train general AI models.

Call recording and your choice

The assistant tells you at the start that the call may be recorded. If you continue, we treat that as your consent. If you do not want to be recorded, say so. The assistant will stop collecting your details and offer to transfer you to the business, or you can call the business back during business hours.

Who we share it with

  • The business you called. They receive a summary and can access the recording and transcript.
  • Speko Group, Inc., our voice AI provider, which hosts the assistant, phone number, recordings and transcripts. Speko is based in the United States and may process and store your information in the United States and other countries. While it is there, it may be accessible to courts and authorities of those countries under their laws.
  • Google (Calendar), only if the business has connected its calendar for bookings.
  • Others where required by law.

Our providers may only use your information to provide services to us.

How long we keep it

  • Call recordings: generally about 30 days on the voice platform, then deleted.
  • Call summaries and transcripts: up to 12 months for quality review and disputes, then deleted.
  • When a business stops using our service, we delete its callers’ information within 30 days unless the law requires us to keep it.

How we protect it

Access is limited to the people who run the service. Accounts use strong passwords and two-factor authentication where available. We do not share recordings in chat apps or by personal email.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the privacy contact at the mailing address above with your phone number and the approximate date of your call. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first. If the business you called is the one that holds your information, we will help them respond.

Complaints

Contact our privacy contact first. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: www.priv.gc.ca, 1-800-282-1376.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new “last updated” date.
