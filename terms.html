Layout Call — AI Receptionist Service Agreement

Last updated: November 11, 2026 (America/Toronto). Terms subject to update.

Provider: Kyle Seguin, sole proprietor, operating as Layout Call, PO Box 1092, 69 Minto Street, Chapleau, ON P0M 1K0, Ontario, Canada. (“we”, “us”, “Layout Call”). Email support details are coming soon.

Client: the Ontario business that signs up for the service (“you”).

The start date and plan (Standard or Growth) are those recorded at signup.

1. The service

1.1 We set up and run an AI phone receptionist (“the Receptionist”) that answers calls you forward to it. It can greet callers, say it is an AI, take messages, collect job details, note booking requests, and attempt to transfer emergency calls to the numbers you give us. It sends you a call summary by email (and by text message once that feature is set up and lawful).

1.2 Setup includes: building the script from your onboarding form, a phone number for forwarding, help with call-forwarding instructions for your carrier, test calls, and go-live.

1.3 Ongoing service includes: hosting through our voice platform provider, weekly review of call logs, script fixes, and email support. Our quality targets are in the Service Standards document. They are targets, not promises.

1.4 The service is delivered remotely. Support and changes are handled by email.

1.5 Not included: answering calls by a human, dispatching technicians, quoting prices, giving any technical, safety, legal or medical advice, outbound sales calls, or service to callers outside Canada.

2. Fees

2.1 Setup fee: CA$149, one time, due at signup.

2.2 Monthly fee: CA$249 per month for the Standard plan, or CA$399 per month for the Growth plan, charged in advance by card through Stripe on the same day each month. Standard includes up to 300 AI call minutes per month. Growth includes about 800 AI call minutes per month.

2.3 Minutes over the plan allowance in a month are billed at CA$0.35 per minute on the next invoice, or we will suggest a plan change. We will email you when you reach 80% of your minutes.

2.4 Fees exclude applicable taxes. If we are registered for GST/HST, we will add HST at the applicable rate.

2.5 If a payment fails, we will email you. If it is not paid within 7 days, we may pause the service until it is paid.

2.6 We may change prices with 30 days’ written (email) notice. You may cancel before the change takes effect.

3. Term and cancellation

3.1 This agreement is month-to-month. There is no minimum term.

3.2 You may cancel at any time by email. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month. Fees already paid are not refunded, except as required by law.

3.3 We may cancel on 30 days’ email notice, or immediately if you breach this agreement, use the service unlawfully, or fail to pay.

3.4 On cancellation, you must remove call forwarding to our number. We will give you instructions. We are not responsible for calls forwarded after the end date.

4. Your responsibilities

4.1 Script approval. Before go-live you must review and approve the greeting and script in writing (email is enough). You must also approve any later change we propose. You are responsible for the accuracy of the facts you give us (hours, service area, services, booking windows, emergency contacts, prices if any).

4.2 Emergency handling is your responsibility. The Receptionist is told to tell callers in danger to hang up and call 911 or their gas utility themselves, and to attempt a transfer to the emergency numbers you provide. The Receptionist never calls 911 or the police. You are responsible for keeping those numbers correct and answered, for responding to emergencies, and for all field work. The Receptionist is not an emergency service, and a transfer can fail (for example, no answer, carrier fault, or platform outage).

4.3 Privacy. You are responsible under Canadian privacy law (including PIPEDA) for the personal information of your callers. You agree to: keep the AI and recording disclosure in the greeting; have a privacy policy that covers this service; use caller information only to serve the caller; and tell us promptly about any caller access or deletion request we must help with.

4.4 You will not use the service for unlawful purposes, outbound telemarketing, calls to or from the United States, or in a setting regulated as health care (for example, under PHIPA) without our written agreement.

4.5 You will keep your call-forwarding set up so that, if our service fails, calls still reach you or your voicemail.

5.2 We will process caller personal information only on your behalf and to provide the service, and protect it with reasonable safeguards.

5.3 We will tell you without undue delay if we learn of a breach of security safeguards involving your callers’ information.

The service may be interrupted, delayed, or make mistakes, including mishearing callers, recording details incorrectly, or failing to transfer. We do not guarantee that the service will be uninterrupted or error-free.

6.3 AI output can be wrong. You should check call summaries and call callers back to confirm details and bookings.
