The problem

You can't pick up with your hands in a furnace, on a roof, under a truck, or on a site. Most callers who reach voicemail don't leave a message. They call the next company on the list.

How it works

1 Your phone rings first.

Calls you don't answer, or all calls after hours, forward to your receptionist.

2 It answers in seconds, 24/7.

It says it's an AI assistant, takes the caller's name, number, location, the problem and how urgent it is, and offers a booking or callback window.

3 You get the details within minutes.

A clear email summary is ready for you to call back — a house call, a shop job, or a site breakdown.

4 Emergencies go straight to you.

For a gas smell, carbon monoxide, fire, flooding or sparking, it tells the caller to call 911 or the gas utility themselves, then tries to put the call through to your cell and your backup. It never calls 911 for them.

FAQ

Do callers know it's an AI?

Yes. It says so at the start of every call and whenever asked. We think that's the right way to do it, and it's part of how we comply with Canadian privacy law.

Are calls recorded?

Calls may be recorded so the details are accurate and so we can review quality. Callers are told up front and can say they'd rather not be recorded. Recordings are generally deleted after about 30 days.

What happens in an emergency?

Layout Call never calls 911 or the police. For gas, carbon monoxide, fire, flooding, sparking or anyone hurt, it tells the caller to hang up and call 911 or the gas utility themselves, and immediately tries to put the call through to you, then your backup number. It never gives safety advice. It isn't an emergency service, and a transfer can fail, so please keep your emergency numbers up to date.

Will it quote prices or diagnose problems?

No. It never makes up prices and never gives repair or technical advice. Quotes come from you.

Does it book jobs into my calendar?

It takes booking requests for the windows you choose and sends them to you to confirm. Direct calendar booking can be added later.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward missed calls to the receptionist line we set up. To stop, you dial one cancel code.

What if it goes down?

We recommend forwarding only missed calls, so your phone still rings first and your voicemail still works. We monitor calls, but like any phone service it can have outages; we don't guarantee uptime.

Where is caller information stored?

Our voice AI provider is based in the U.S. and may process data in the U.S. and other countries. See our Privacy Policy. We never sell caller information or use it for marketing.

Is there a contract?

Month-to-month. Cancel any time by email; service runs to the end of the paid month. The current Service Agreement is on this site. Terms are subject to update.

Do you do sales calls?

No. Everything is online and by email, so you can try the demo, sign up, and get help without booking a meeting.

Does it work for industrial or mining contractors, not just home service?

Yes. The same receptionist takes missed and after-hours calls for shops, residential service, and industrial or mining service work — breakdowns, site calls, parts, and dispatch — and sends you the details. You approve the script, including how site and emergency calls are handled.
